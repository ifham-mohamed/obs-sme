## 2. Why Rare-Domain Top-Up Was Needed

The 800-row v1 dataset was usable for the first training-preparation pass, but rare categories were too sparse for a reliable final classifier claim:

```text
SECTOR_SPECIFIC          671
TAX_RATE_CHANGE           56
IMPORT_EXPORT             32
LABOUR_LAW                27
BUSINESS_REGISTRATION      5
PENALTY_ENFORCEMENT        5
PRODUCT_STANDARD           4
EPF_ETF_CHANGE             0
```

The v1 Kaggle LoRA diagnostics did not beat the TF-IDF baseline. The evidence pointed to data imbalance, not just tuning, as the next real problem. Therefore, Batch 06 and Batch 07 were created to collect official rare-domain examples.

## 8. Interpretation For The Final Report

Use this statement unless a later model run supersedes it:

> Rare-domain top-up increased the Module 1 gold dataset from 800 to 1128 labelled regulation records and substantially improved category balance for product, penalty, labour, import/export, tax, and business-registration categories. Inter-annotator agreement improved to category kappa 0.9472, mean sector kappa 0.9656, and SME relevance kappa 0.9146. On the v3 stratified split, TF-IDF LinearSVC achieved 0.9080 macro-F1, close to but still below the 0.92 target. The remaining bottlenecks are `EPF_ETF_CHANGE`, which still has only 11 total examples, and `PENALTY_ENFORCEMENT`, which has lower held-out F1 than other rare classes.

## 9. Important Limitations

- Batch 06 and Batch 07 were assisted/direct Label Studio annotations using rule-based preparation logic, not a strict manual click-through process for every row. The labels are usable for development evidence, but strict research reporting should state this method.
- If a stricter evidence standard is required, Batch 06 and Batch 07 should be manually reviewed in Label Studio before final thesis submission.
- `EPF_ETF_CHANGE` remains sparse even after Batch 07: 11 total rows, 7 train rows, 2 validation rows, and 2 test rows.
- The v3 LinearSVC score is close to the gate but still not a final pass.
- No XLM-R LoRA checkpoint should be promoted unless it beats the v3 TF-IDF LinearSVC baseline on the same split and passes slice checks.
