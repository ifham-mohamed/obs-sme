Architecture & pipeline flow

A gazette moves through: scrape → extract → preprocess → classify → (summarize → alert → lag). Everything up to classify is built; summarize/alert/lag are Phase 4–5.

documents.gov.lk ──Scrapy──▶ [ingested] ──extract_gazette──▶ [extracted] ──preprocess_gazette──▶ [preprocessed] ──classify_gazette──▶ [classified] ┄┄▶ (alerts · propagation lag · findings)
